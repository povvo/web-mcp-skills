# Brand Expression

Output path: `foundations/brand-expression.md`

## Meaning System

- Core meaning: **inspectable structure**. The system makes paths, boundaries, state, scale, and evidence visible without drowning the user in chrome.
- Secondary meanings: disciplined launch energy, operational precision, editorial confidence, and controlled transformation.
- The language refuses to mean “magic AI,” retro terminal, cyberpunk network, rocket company, military command system, or a copied identity. “Web MCP” is the displayed name; it does not supply the visual motifs.

## Motifs And Signature Patterns

- Motifs: original path and node, bounded one-bit signal field, measurement rail, clipped JetBrains Mono anchor, black/white inversion, and sparse dot residue.
- Repeated gestures: expose a route, mark a decision, label the state, then leave it still.
- Signature layouts: small factual copy against one monumental but dim crop; rigorous grid with one edge escape; dense media beside a quiet text field.
- Signature interactions: line trace from action to result, open-to-filled node state, selected-region inversion, and a durable factual completion label.

## Emotional Contract

- The promise is calm control at the edge of something powerful: users see what can act, what is acting, and what happened.
- Trust is evidence-led and non-anthropomorphic. Unknown, pending, failed, and completed remain visually and verbally distinct.
- Intensity ranges from nearly silent editorial pages to one bounded high-signal reveal. The baseline is never continuously loud.
- Delight comes from exact scale shifts, responsive crop, and causal reveal—not jokes, colour, bounce, or spectacle detached from state.

## Semiotic Controls

- Paths mean relationship or progress; nodes mean state, decision, endpoint, or inspected object. Do not assign an abstract meaning without a label.
- Black/white inversion means emphasis or mode change; graphite means recessive structure. Status adds pattern, shape, and text.
- JetBrains Mono means measurable, inspectable, and exact. Monumental size adds identity; small uppercase adds metadata.
- Matte grain means physical reproduction and tactility; halftone means compressed signal or media translation.
- Cultural risks include reading arrow-like motifs as nationalist, military, upload, or religious symbols. Avoid inherited emblems and validate original marks across target locales.

## Adaptation Boundaries

- Product-specific nouns, data, screenshots, diagram topology, display word, and density may change.
- Navigation conventions, target sizes, pointer/gesture affordances, font delivery, and responsive rails adapt to platform.
- JetBrains Mono, neutral-only base palette, micro/macro hierarchy, one expressive rupture, quiet/signal alternation, original path/node semantics, and source-asset independence stay fixed.

## Product-Domain Extension

- Vocabulary comes only from the verified product brief. The visual language contributes no product nouns, actors, objects, processes, or technical terminology.
- Product modules may compose an object index, operation trace, evidence inspector, boundary, or result record only when the actual domain model defines them.
- A real finite operation may use nodes for prepared/running/completed and a path for causality; a verified object detail may use a quiet data grid beside one bounded signal preview.
- Do not borrow browser logos, network-blue palettes, chain links, plugs, AI sparkles, green terminal text, or orbital/rocket imagery merely because of the product name.

## Translation Rules

- Name the real state, then choose the smallest motif that exposes it.
- For brand-only applications, use the word “WEB MCP” in JetBrains Mono and original geometry; do not construct a proprietary glyph imitation.
- Across channels, repeat two signature cues and preserve one large quiet region rather than forcing a complete motif checklist.

## Anti-Patterns

- Copied marks or letterforms, literal rockets, generic protocol diagrams, unsupported claims of speed or intelligence, decorative pseudo-data, all-over halftone, colourful tech gradients, animated node wallpaper, or an identity so abstract that users cannot read the name.
