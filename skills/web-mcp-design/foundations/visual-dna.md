# Visual Design DNA

Output path: `foundations/visual-dna.md`


## Core Visual Territory

- Named territory: **Instrumented Monochrome**—a severe editorial field made legible through inspectable geometry and measured data residue.
- Boundary of the territory: remain neutral, planar, typographic, and diagrammatic. It may feel engineered, but never like conventional developer tooling, science fiction chrome, or a generic terminal theme.
- Primary visual forces: black/white polarity, disproportionate scale, asymmetric voids, modular line paths, circular nodes, binary raster texture, clipped edges, and small coordinate-like labels.
- What must be recognizable at a glance: a single immense typographic or geometric anchor held inside a mostly quiet field, with fine operational evidence nearby.

## Dominant Emotional Register

- Register name: controlled launch tension.
- Emotional temperature: cold and matte, with no colourful warmth.
- Tension pattern: near-silence interrupted by one high-energy crop, inversion, diagonal, or signal field.
- Trust cues: visible structure, exact labels, stable alignment, terse status copy, and deterministic transitions. Delight comes from scale and reveal, not whimsy.

## Repeated Grammar

| Grammar signal | How it repeats | Where it controls the system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Binary field | near-black and off-white invert by mode or region | palette, surfaces, states, imagery |
| Inspectable path | thin orthogonal/diagonal routes terminate or turn at round nodes | focus, diagrams, progress, navigation |
| Extreme scale interval | micro labels sit against cropped display type or symbols | typography, hierarchy, hero composition |
| Bounded raster | one rectangular field converts image or data into dots | media, loading, transitions, empty states |
| Deliberate overflow | one anchor exceeds its container while functional content stays aligned | layout, campaigns, headers, data views |
| Measurement residue | coordinates, counts, dimensions, or state codes appear at edges | metadata, diagnostics, captions |

## Invariants

- JetBrains Mono is the only type family. Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, tracking, case, rotation, and crop.
- Use neutral colour only. Meaning is never encoded by hue alone.
- Every node, line, dot field, number, or crop needs an information or compositional role.
- Preserve at least one substantial quiet region. The language collapses when every region competes.

## Allowed Variation

- Density may switch between `quiet` and `signal`; theme may invert; media may use coarse or fine halftone; the giant anchor may be word, numeral, diagram, or original symbol.
- Mobile layouts may remove rotation and reduce overflow, but must preserve micro/macro contrast and a visible path or measurement cue.

## Contradictions To Preserve

- Machine precision against tactile grain; monumental scale against tiny factual copy; rigid grids against one clipped escape; dense signal against extensive silence.

## Anti-Patterns

- Neon protocol colours, gradients, glassmorphism, soft rounded card farms, faux-terminal green, random circuit traces, decorative node clouds, noisy glitch effects, stock futuristic imagery, cute icons, excessive animation, or a custom techno alphabet.
- Do not fill empty space merely to appear technical. Do not use halftone behind body copy. Do not turn every corner into a chamfer.
