Output path: `sources/source-mode-map.md`

## Mode Handling

| Source mode | Source handling | Watch for | Output implications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand artifacts | Extract repeated construction logic from marks, typography specimens, campaign frames, and editorial identity layouts. Preserve expression rather than brand strategy. | Do not reproduce the source wordmark, emblem, claims, coordinates, copy, or implied business positioning. Do not treat one presentation trick as a universal rule. | Supports the monochrome palette, geometric line/node motif, cropped type scale, measurement metadata, binary imagery, and quiet editorial voice. |
| Moodboard | Compare all four frames and promote only repeated or mutually reinforcing signals. Treat black/white inversion, sparse fields, extreme scale, data-like annotation, and controlled rasterization as the shared grammar. | Watch for outlier inflation: the dense halftone field and exposed vector skeleton are conditional modes, not mandatory decoration on every surface. | Establishes two density states: sparse operational canvas and bounded signal field. Provides drift boundaries for texture and composition. |
| Written brief | Apply the exact destination, name, use case, font, and isolation constraints supplied by the user. Translate product context only into the displayed name. | “Web MCP” must not introduce browser chrome, protocol diagrams, network-blue accents, generic AI gradients, or a technical-dashboard aesthetic unsupported by the images. | Names the skill `web-mcp-design`, requires JetBrains Mono, and keeps all generated files under the requested skill root. |

## Excluded Modes

The corpus does not support UI-screen, product-or-packaging, physical-environment, generated-image-set, or visual-narrative claims. Component, interaction, motion, accessibility, and platform rules are therefore careful reconstructions from the extracted visual principles and common web behavior, not observations of source interfaces. Those areas must remain compatible with the language without pretending they were directly depicted.

## Translation Boundary

Every design-use rule should survive removal of the source-specific names and imagery. Source handling is complete when an implementer can produce a new Web MCP surface using JetBrains Mono, monochrome contrast, inspectable geometry, asymmetric negative space, and bounded halftone texture without recreating any protected mark or source composition.
