# Instrumented Monochrome

## Use Contract

Instrumented Monochrome is a source-independent design language for identity, editorial, interface, data, motion, and generated visual work. It is named for its two defining qualities: severe neutral fields and visible evidence of construction. It can be used by a designer, engineer, motion artist, image generator, or Codex instance without hidden context. The language does not prescribe a product model. “Web MCP” is the name displayed when an artifact requires a name; those words contribute no browser, protocol, server, network, developer-tool, or artificial-intelligence motifs.

Every correct output must feel controlled, inspectable, and poised. A mostly quiet field supports one dense or monumental event. Functional content is aligned and readable. Expressive content may cross a boundary once. Geometry communicates paths, endpoints, state, measurement, or containment rather than decorating empty space. Motion exposes causality and then settles.

Non-negotiables:

- The language name is exactly **Instrumented Monochrome**. The applied product name is **Web MCP** when a name is required.
- JetBrains Mono is the only designed type family. Native monospace is an emergency loading fallback, not a second voice.
- The base palette is achromatic: near-black, paper, signal white, and graphite intermediates. No hue is introduced merely because the subject sounds technical.
- Use a rigorous grid for useful content and permit no more than one nonessential crop, rotation, inversion, diagonal, or overflow rupture per major composition.
- Default to a quiet field. Concentrate halftone, dense data, or repeated marks inside one bounded signal region.
- Paths connect real relationships; nodes encode real state or mark a purposeful construction point. Measurements, IDs, counts, and coordinates must be real or clearly nonsemantic specimen labels.
- Build from named tokens, component recipes, and pattern rules before inventing new choices.
- Never rely on colour, motion, texture, position, tiny text, or an icon alone as the only carrier of required information.
- Do not reuse a known mark, wordmark, image, copy block, or composition. Production geometry and texture must be original or procedural.

## Design DNA

Core territory:

1. **Instrumented field** — a matte plane that exposes a small amount of meaningful geometry and metadata.
2. **Editorial scale tension** — micro labels against monumental JetBrains Mono words, numerals, or identifiers.
3. **Binary signal** — bounded inversion or one-bit raster that concentrates activity without flooding the frame.
4. **Measured rupture** — one deliberately off-grid crop, diagonal, or rotation that returns to alignment immediately afterward.

Emotional register:

The governing feeling is controlled launch tension: cold, exact, watchful, and ready to act. The system is not sterile. Restrained grain and coarse raster give it tactile reproduction, while exact paths and typography keep it trustworthy. Intensity ranges from nearly silent editorial space to one decisive reveal. Avoid cyberpunk, playfulness, conversational friendliness, warm nostalgia, luxury gloss, bureaucratic card density, and spectacle detached from state.

Signature grammar:

- Near-black and off-white fields with graphite for recessive structure.
- JetBrains Mono spanning 11px measured labels to viewport-scale cropped anchors.
- One-pixel orthogonal paths, occasional 45-degree transitions, small-radius turns, and circular nodes.
- One bounded halftone, data, image, or selected region.
- Substantial negative space and off-axis alignment.
- Edge metadata containing real state, count, dimension, time, or identifier.
- Brief node-path-node motion followed by stillness.
- Factual microcopy that distinguishes pending, known, failed, partial, and complete.

Core principles:

- **Make structure inspectable.** Show the smallest route, boundary, state, or measurement that explains the object.
- **Align the useful; rupture the expressive.** Reading order, controls, and evidence stay stable while one nonessential anchor may break the grid.
- **Use polarity before colour.** Hierarchy comes from black/white inversion, lightness, line, fill, pattern, type, and space.
- **Alternate silence and signal.** A dense region earns attention by sitting beside quiet space.
- **Let scale do the branding.** Recognition comes from the JetBrains Mono micro/monument interval, not a custom techno alphabet.
- **Settle after consequence.** Feedback is immediate and causal, then durable and still.

Primary anti-patterns are gradients, neon accents, glassmorphism, glows, soft shadow card farms, pill controls, terminal green, random circuit traces, rockets, plugs, chain links, globes, robots, magic sparkles, decorative node clouds, fake coordinates, all-over halftone, custom techno lettering, ambient motion, tiny grey copy, and source-derived marks.

Outlier handling:

Dense raster, exposed construction skeletons, vertical text, and massive crops are bounded variants rather than defaults. A product screen may be fully quiet and still belong if it retains neutral polarity, JetBrains Mono, disciplined alignment, exact state language, and one measured edge. Do not force every signature into every component.

Establishment and subversion:

- Establish palette, type, grid, state, and surface behavior for at least two visible alignments before any break.
- Treat the break as one bounded event. The following component or scroll region returns to quiet alignment.
- Use an approximate 8:1 establishment-to-subversion ratio: eight stable decisions for one rupture.
- Reject any subversion that weakens reading order, contrast, focus, truthful content, platform behavior, or product transfer.

## Reconstructive Fidelity

Canonical frame types:

1. **Identity field:** “Web MCP” as a live, oversized JetBrains Mono anchor, one edge crop, one measured label rail, and a mostly empty neutral canvas.
2. **Quiet editorial page:** small metadata, readable body block, strong heading interval, one long rule, no enclosing card.
3. **Signal split:** quiet text or controls beside one bounded inverse, halftone, media, or dense-data field.
4. **Inspectable route:** labelled nodes and paths representing a real finite sequence, relationship, or state transition.
5. **Evidence inspector:** aligned object list or data grid paired with one selected inverse detail region.
6. **Campaign plate:** one monumental type or original symbol crop with small factual labels and target-specific texture.

Geometry, crop, and lighting:

Functional geometry is frontal and planar. Use horizontal/vertical axes, one 45-degree transition where direction or transformation needs it, and 2-8px radii only where a path or control turns. Circular nodes are true circles. Essential content stays inside `clamp(16px, 4vw, 72px)` safe edges. A display anchor may exceed one edge, but its identifying core remains visible and its crop never changes DOM order. Depth comes from tonal steps, opaque overlap, scale, and inversion—not shadow or blur. Light is flat, neutral, and hard-edged. White appears luminous by contrast rather than glow.

Source-free reconstruction recipe:

1. Choose the truthful use case and canonical frame. Obtain the real content, states, actions, and evidence before selecting motifs.
2. Set a near-black or paper canvas, the responsive grid, a quiet/signal density, and JetBrains Mono roles.
3. Align all functional content. Choose one dominant anchor and, only if needed, one bounded signal region.
4. Add paths, nodes, measurements, media, or texture only when each element has a real semantic or compositional role.
5. Bind controls and state to semantic tokens and documented component recipes. Define loading, error, recovery, completion, focus, high contrast, and reduced motion.
6. Apply at most one rupture. Remove every second crop, diagonal, rotation, or high-density competitor.
7. Test live text, reading order, contrast, type rendering, device-pixel rules, crop, responsive modes, keyboard behavior, and source independence.

Replication checks:

- Palette fidelity passes only when all designed colours are neutral roles and every informative pair meets its contrast obligation.
- Type fidelity passes only when JetBrains Mono carries every role, body remains readable, and monumental type is nonessential or fully available elsewhere.
- Spatial fidelity passes only when useful content is aligned, a quiet region survives, and no more than one rupture and one signal field compete.
- Surface fidelity passes only when fields are matte/opaque, texture is bounded away from text, and no glow, glass, gradient, or shadow supplies hierarchy.
- Interaction fidelity passes only when states are labelled, focus is independent, progress is truthful, recovery preserves work, and reduced motion preserves meaning.

## Palette System

Palette thesis: an optically cool achromatic range uses near-black/paper polarity for structure and graphite for recession; brightness is semantic priority rather than decoration.

Semantic tokens:

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `neutral.black` | `#0A0A0A` | dark canvas and light-mode strong ink |
| `neutral.raised-dark` | `#141414` | one-step dark raised surface |
| `neutral.graphite` | `#3A3A3A` | dim display crop and inactive structure |
| `neutral.mid` | `#777777` | nonessential light-mode structure after contrast review |
| `neutral.paper` | `#E8E8E4` | dark-mode readable ink and light raised surface |
| `neutral.white` | `#F7F7F2` | light canvas, active node, bounded signal endpoint |

Contrast obligations:

- Primary text/canvas: 4.5:1 minimum for ordinary text, preferably 7:1 for body after dark-mode halation review.
- Large text: 3:1 minimum only when it meets the size/weight definition; monumental decorative crops may be lower only if hidden and repeated accessibly.
- Focus, essential icon, control boundary, and graphical state: 3:1 against adjacent colours.
- Status and selection always retain a label plus node, boundary, or pattern backup.

Forbidden uses include accent hues, red/green status, transparent semantic fields, gradients, coloured photography left untreated, grey-on-grey required copy, large pure-white dark-mode flashes, and texture that lowers text or focus contrast.

## Typography System

Type thesis: JetBrains Mono behaves as factual infrastructure at reading scale and architectural identity at display scale; hierarchy comes from scale, weight, tracking, case, placement, and crop within one family.

Type roles:

| Role | Family | Weight | Size range | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | JetBrains Mono | 600-700 | `clamp(4rem, 16vw, 18rem)` | nonessential anchor, name, numeral, section identity |
| Heading | JetBrains Mono | 600 | `clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 4.5rem)` | concise section/title hierarchy |
| Body | JetBrains Mono | 400 | `1rem-1.125rem` | prose, instructions, values |
| Label | JetBrains Mono | 500 | `0.6875rem-0.8125rem` | metadata, state, caption, measurement |
| Utility | JetBrains Mono | 500-600 | `0.8125rem-1rem` | controls, navigation, compact data |

Scale:

- Base: 16px at a 16px root; respect user scaling.
- Scale logic: hand-tuned micro/body/heading/display intervals rather than a single mathematical ratio.
- Tracking: display `-0.05em` with `-0.07em` floor; body normal; labels `0.06em-0.12em` uppercase.
- Line height: display `0.78-0.88`; heading `0.95-1.05`; body `1.5-1.65`; labels `1.25-1.4`.
- Maximum body line length: 72 characters; preferred range 45-72.

Microtype rules:

- Use sentence case for body/headings and uppercase for short metadata labels only.
- Use tabular figures for comparable numbers, timestamps, counters, and aligned measurements.
- Never set required interface text below 11px or body below 16px.
- Keep essential text live/selectable. Do not distort glyphs into symbols; build original SVG geometry separately.
- Web delivery should preload used weights only and use `font-display: swap`. Verify `0/O`, `1/l/I`, punctuation, dark-mode stroke retention, and crop on target engines.
- Native substitutions are allowed only when the family cannot be delivered, and the result needs explicit metric/voice review.

## Spatial System

Space thesis: a disciplined responsive grid aligns useful information while active negative space and one controlled overflow create figure-ground tension.

Base unit: 4px. Grid: 4 columns on narrow viewports, 8 at medium widths, and 12 at wide widths. Use 16-72px responsive margins and 16-32px gutters as content requires. Breakpoints follow content failure rather than device names. Density modes are `quiet` and `signal`, with compact layout permitted for real data comparison only.

Spacing scale:

- xs: 4px.
- sm: 8px.
- md: 16px.
- lg: 24px.
- xl: 32px.
- 2xl: 48px.
- field: 72px.
- section: `clamp(48px, 9vw, 144px)`.

Composition rules:

- Align body, headings, controls, labels, and evidence to shared grid lines. Place edge labels 4-8px from what they describe.
- Preserve roughly 55-75% quiet space in identity/editorial frames and at least 25-40% in task-dense views.
- Use one focal object and one clear first/second/third reading order. The largest object may be dim while smaller functional copy holds higher contrast.
- Group with proximity before frames. Use one-pixel rules, tonal steps, or inversion before cards.
- Crop one edge of one nonessential display object. Remove vertical labels and reduce overflow on mobile before shrinking readable text.
- Maintain 44px touch targets, 32px compact pointer targets, and 8px between adjacent targets irrespective of visible density.

## Surface And Material System

Surface thesis: matte screen-black and uncoated paper fields carry crisp plotted geometry and restrained one-bit reproduction, creating tactile weight without elevation effects.

Surface roles:

| Surface | Description | Implementation approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary field | near-solid matte neutral | opaque background; optional stable grain below 2% apparent contrast |
| Ground | flat content plane | spacing and alignment, no shadow |
| Raised | one neutral step or one-pixel frame | `#141414` dark / `#E8E8E4` light |
| Overlay | fully opaque inverse or canvas field | clear edge, managed focus, no backdrop blur |
| Critical | bounded high-contrast field | double rule or diagonal hatch plus label; no hue dependency |

Texture rules:

- Quiet grain is optional, stable, non-directional, and imperceptible over text. It is not a screenshot of paper.
- Signal halftone uses 4-10px cells and 20-70% dot coverage inside one bounded media or data region.
- Mask texture at least 4px away from body copy, small labels, icons, paths, and focus.
- Generate grain/halftone procedurally with deterministic output. Test physical dot gain in print and device-scale sharpness on screen.
- Allow sparse dot dropout inside generated media, never damage to functional geometry.

Material vocabulary: dry matte field, plotted line, one-bit print raster, opaque ink, graphite construction, hard frontal light, uncoated paper, and precise industrial reproduction. Forbid chrome, glass, blur, gloss, bevel, glow, gradient mesh, film damage, scratches, torn edges, and heavy grunge.

## Motion System

Motion thesis: short mechanical responses expose where state travelled, then settle into still evidence.

Duration tokens:

- instant: 90ms for press, node fill, or inversion response.
- fast: 140ms for hover and compact exits.
- default: 240ms for path tracing and field settlement.
- slow: 320ms maximum for major ordinary structural transitions.

Easing:

- Enter: `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` for a large field settling into alignment.
- Exit: `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)` for a faster collapse toward the source action.
- In-place: `cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)` for path/state continuity; `steps(1,end)` for decisive inversion.

Motion rules:

- Use node → path → node choreography for a real relation or sequence, with labels appearing at the destination.
- Stagger at most five related items by 30-50ms, with total delayed time below 240ms.
- Keep reading surfaces, giant background type, construction grids, and completed states still.
- Loading appears by roughly 300ms and uses a real step/value or one labelled current node. Do not crawl dots across the entire field.
- Error stops and persists. Success closes the endpoint once and remains as a result.
- User input may interrupt and retarget motion; do not queue cinematic sequences.
- Reduced motion shows the complete final path, node, label, and inversion immediately. Translation, tracing, stagger, threshold animation, parallax, zoom, shake, cursor following, and repeated flicker stop.

## Interaction And States

Feedback density: one local geometry change, one factual state label, and one optional causal transition are enough for most actions. Withhold decoration, never required state, consequence, or recovery.

State anatomy:

| State | Visual change | Copy behavior | Accessibility rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Default | open node/quiet boundary | concrete noun or verb | native semantic and readable label |
| Hover | boundary/underline strengthens | unchanged | optional pointer cue; not sole affordance |
| Focus | independent 2px offset outline/path | unchanged | `focus-visible`, 3:1, unobscured, logical order |
| Active | node closes or compact region inverts for 90ms | original action remains | native press/key behavior; one activation |
| Selected | complete bounded region inverts or current node fills | selected/current label remains | programmatic selected/current state distinct from focus |
| Disabled | readable recessive state, no pointer response | reason when non-obvious | native disabled behavior; do not disable readable data |
| Loading | labelled current path/node and real value if known | present tense, exact step/count | polite announcement, duplicate action prevented |
| Error | double rule/hatch and stop node | what failed, retained result, repair | persistent; linked message; scoped retry |
| Warning | double open node and assertive boundary | specific consequence | label and confirmation proportional to risk |
| Success | closed endpoint and stable path | result, count/ID/time when real | durable state; no colour/motion-only meaning |

Silence rule: routine navigation and low-risk completion do not require toast, sound, animation, or praise. The interface avoids seeming broken through immediate press feedback, a processing label by 300ms, and a durable result or scoped failure. Sound is off by default; haptics are optional host conventions with visible equivalents.

## Component Recipes

### Identity Field

Intent: name an artifact or brand surface without implying product behavior. Anatomy: live “Web MCP” text, one neutral field, one measured-edge label group, optional original path, optional bounded signal region. Construction: JetBrains Mono display at `clamp(4rem,16vw,18rem)`, one edge crop, 40-70% quiet area, no embedded essential text. Variants: light/dark, wide/portrait, quiet/signal. Forbidden: copied marks, rockets, protocol diagrams, multiple crops, custom glyphs, or product claims inferred from the name.

### Primary Action

Intent: perform the one main action in a region. Anatomy: opaque inverse container, specific live label, optional familiar icon, focus outline, optional processing node. Construction: 44px target, square/4px turn, 500 weight label, 90ms press, semantic field/text tokens. Variants: primary, secondary outline, tertiary text, destructive double-boundary. States: default, hover, focus, pressed, loading, disabled, error. Forbidden: icon-only abstract action, pill, glow, raw colour, label replacement by spinner, or optimistic success.

### Field

Intent: collect or select one truthful value. Anatomy: persistent label, native control, optional prefix/suffix, helper, count, linked error. Construction: canvas surface, one-pixel boundary, 2px independent focus, 16px value, 11-13px metadata, preserved input on failure. Variants: standard, dense, code/data, search, choice. Forbidden: placeholder-only label, colour-only validation, custom keyboard-hostile select, clearing failed input, or decorative floating label.

### Inspectable Route

Intent: show a real finite relationship, sequence, or operation. Anatomy: ordered labels, open/filled/double/stop nodes, connectors, current state, value/time if real, recovery. Construction: one-pixel orthogonal/45-degree path, 2px state emphasis, 240ms causal trace, static reduced-motion equivalent. Variants: step route, operation trace, determinate meter, static history. Forbidden: fake percent, unlabeled dots, decorative branches, ambient crawling path, or completion without owning evidence.

### Status Field

Intent: communicate information, loading, warning, failure, partial result, or completion. Anatomy: state node, title/message, retained evidence, optional details, one primary recovery action. Construction: raised or bounded inverse field, factual copy, semantic geometry/pattern, persistent error, live-region policy. Variants: informative, processing, warning, error, success, empty. Forbidden: generic error, auto-dismissed critical state, repeated announcements, celebration, or hue-only status.

### Evidence Inspector

Intent: let users scan a collection and inspect one selected object. Anatomy: semantic list/table, factual labels/values, one selected inverse detail region, copy/raw affordance, optional truthful media. Construction: quiet grid, tabular figures, low-priority rules, maximum one signal region, logical DOM order. Variants: editorial, dense comparison, raw data, media preview. Forbidden: card around every item, fake fields, truncated uncopyable IDs, multiple selected panels, colour-only charts, or texture beneath data.

## Accessibility

Contrast compliance:

- Normal text minimum: 4.5:1.
- Large text minimum: 3:1.
- UI components and graphical objects: 3:1 minimum when needed to understand/operate.
- Primary body prefers 7:1 where dark-mode halation remains acceptable.
- Focus uses a 2px offset strong-ink/Highlight outline at 3:1 minimum.

Colour independence: the base system uses no hue categories. Pending/current/complete/warning/error/disabled use label, open/filled/double/stop node, boundary, hatch, and programmatic state. Removing colour must lose no information.

Focus indicators: use a two-pixel offset rectangular/path outline in strong ink or system Highlight. It remains distinct from hover, selected, error, and loading, is not clipped, and scrolls into view with breathing space.

Motion: honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and native settings. Replace tracing, translation, stagger, threshold animation, and field settlement with immediate final geometry and labels. Avoid parallax, large zoom, shake, flicker, cursor followers, and white flashes.

Alt text stance: describe the purpose and information of meaningful images, not their decorative treatment. Halftone/grain is empty-alt. Charts and diagrams need summaries/data. Preserve an unprocessed alternative when transformation removes decisive evidence.

Minimum text size: 16px body and 11px metadata at default zoom, with 200% text zoom and applicable 400% reflow. Touch targets are 44px/pt minimum and preferably 48dp on Android.

## Drift Boundaries

This system has drifted if any of these conditions are met:

- Palette drift: any intentional hue, gradient, glow, transparent semantic field, or failed required contrast appears.
- Type drift: a designed family other than JetBrains Mono appears, essential type is distorted/rasterized, body drops below 16px, or labels below 11px carry meaning.
- Spatial drift: functional content is off-grid, multiple ruptures/signal regions compete, quiet space disappears, or essential crop changes reading order.
- Surface drift: shadow, blur, glass, gloss, heavy distress, or texture beneath text supplies hierarchy.
- Motion drift: ambient or repeated motion appears, routine transitions exceed 500ms, completion depends on animation, or reduced motion loses meaning.
- Interaction drift: hover replaces focus, state lacks labels/recovery, progress is fabricated, errors auto-dismiss, or completion is claimed without evidence.
- Component drift: a component references raw values, invents unsupported states/props, drops keyboard/accessibility behavior, or mimics a product model inferred from the name.
- Prompt drift: an asset contains copied marks/compositions, product clichés, hue, multiple focal regions, extra/malformed text, fake data, or source-image dependence.
- Accessibility drift: focus is removed/obscured, contrast/targets/reflow fail, state is colour/motion-only, or live feedback steals focus/repeats excessively.

## One-Line Brief

Build a cold, source-independent monochrome instrument: JetBrains Mono at radical scale, useful content rigorously aligned, one bounded signal or rupture, meaningful paths and state nodes, tactile one-bit texture, factual feedback, and decisive stillness.
