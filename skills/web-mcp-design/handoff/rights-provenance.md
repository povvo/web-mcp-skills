## Use Boundaries

- Reference-only material: R01-R04 and their visible marks, lettering, copy, imagery, composition, and pixel texture. They validate extraction decisions but are not runtime assets.
- Production-cleared material: no third-party visual asset or font binary was cleared by this task. The generated source-free documentation is usable within the requested workspace, subject to repository licensing decisions outside this extraction.
- Restricted material: reference pixels and recognizable source identity elements must not be copied, traced, embedded, or used in prompts for production imitation.
- Unknown-rights material: any later generated asset, external icon library, photograph, product screenshot, logo candidate, or font package requires its own Custody, Rights, licence, and Production use record.

## Provenance Rule

Design-use files contain operational consequences only. Source identifiers and rights records stay here and in `sources/`/`proofs/`. A future artifact should be reconstructible from `DESIGN.md` without loading R01-R04. Hash/source matching is a review aid, not proof of legal ownership or non-infringement.
